🎙️ Cerebrus AI Voice Chat

📋 Instructions:

  1. Copy the App ID, Channel, and Token from your Streamlit app
  2. Paste them into the fields above
  3. Click "Join Voice Chat"
  4. Allow microphone access when prompted
  5. Start talking to your AI assistant!
  6. The AI will respond with voice synthesis

💡 Tips:

  • Speak clearly and wait for responses
  • Ask questions about your uploaded documents (if RAG is enabled)
  • Say "goodbye" to end the conversation naturally
  • Make sure your microphone is working
